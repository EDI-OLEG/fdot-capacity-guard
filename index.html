FAQ

The obvious questions.

Is this an FDOT service?

No. FDOT Capacity Guard is an independent managed service and is not affiliated with the Florida Department of Transportation.

Do you need our FDOT credentials?

No. The service is designed around exports, spreadsheets and evidence supplied by the contractor. Your authorized user retains official CPQ access and submission control.

Is the capacity number an official FDOT determination?

No. The pack is an internal operating and reconciliation view. Official bidding capacity, prequalification status and certification must be confirmed in the contractor's current FDOT records.

What do you need to start?

A current contract/work-underway export or spreadsheet, the current working Work Underway total, current Maximum Bidding Capacity, the status date and any known fresh awards/pending awards.

Why not just use our spreadsheet?

If the spreadsheet stays current with little effort, you probably should. The service is for teams where ownership is fragmented and the real cost is chasing updates, reconciling differences and checking capacity before the next bid.

OFFICIAL BASIS

Anchored to FDOT's contractor-prequalification workflow.

FDOT says prequalified contractors certify total Work Underway at least once every 30 days; its instructions say Total Uncompleted Work includes work performed as prime or subcontractor. FDOT also describes entering Total Uncompleted Work and attaching a Status of Contracts on Hand template or an equivalent system file.
